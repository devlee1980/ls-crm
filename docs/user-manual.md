**LifeScientific Customer Relationship Management Platform**

Version 1.0 — May 2026

## Welcome

LS Nexus is the LifeScientific sales platform. It connects you to your customers, your forecasts, your action items, and your product catalogue in one place. This manual walks you through every screen and feature you'll encounter as a Sales Representative, Sales Manager, or Administrator.

If you're new to LS Nexus, read the first three sections (Getting Started, Multi-Factor Authentication, Navigating LS Nexus) before doing anything else. After that, you can jump to whichever feature section you need.

### Who this manual is for

- **Sales Representatives (REP)** — manage customers assigned to you, build forecasts, log action items, track deals through the pipeline.
- **Sales Managers (MANAGER)** — everything a rep can do, plus see all customers and forecasts in your division (US or Canada).
- **Administrators (ADMIN)** — everything a manager can do, plus user provisioning, MFA resets, and cross-division visibility.

Throughout this manual, role-specific notes look like this:

> **ADMIN only** — Some features are restricted to administrators. They are clearly called out.

## 1. Getting Started

### 1.1 What you need

- A laptop or desktop computer with a modern web browser. LS Nexus works best in **Chrome**, **Edge**, **Firefox**, or **Safari** (latest two major versions).
- A smartphone with an authenticator app installed. Any of the following work:
  - Google Authenticator
  - Microsoft Authenticator
  - 1Password
  - Authy
  - Duo Mobile
- An email address provisioned by the LS Nexus administrator. You can't self-register; an administrator must create your account first.

### 1.2 Your first sign-in

1. Go to **https://www.ls-nexus.com**.
2. Enter the email address and temporary password you received from your administrator.
3. Click **Sign In**.
4. You will be redirected automatically to **/mfa/enroll**. Complete enrollment as described in [Section 2](#2-multi-factor-authentication-mfa).
5. After MFA enrollment finishes, you land on the **Dashboard**.

### 1.3 Changing your temporary password

After your first successful sign-in, change your password as soon as possible:

1. The current implementation uses temporary passwords set by your administrator. To change yours, sign out, go to the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page, and follow the email-based reset flow described in [Section 3.2](#32-forgot-password-flow).

### 1.4 Signing out

LS Nexus times out automatically after **8 hours** of session inactivity. To sign out manually, click your avatar in the top-right corner of any page and choose **Sign Out**.

## 2. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

LS Nexus requires every user to use an authenticator app on their phone. There is no way to skip this; the platform is gated by federal data-protection requirements applicable to LifeScientific's customer information.

### 2.1 What MFA is and why we use it

Multi-factor authentication means proving you are who you say you are using **two** independent factors:

1. Something you know: your password.
2. Something you have: a 6-digit code generated by your phone every 30 seconds.

If someone steals your password, they cannot sign in without also stealing your phone. If someone steals your phone, they cannot use it without your password.

The code is generated by an algorithm called **TOTP** (Time-based One-Time Password). It does not require an internet connection on your phone — your phone simply needs the correct time.

### 2.2 First-time enrollment

The first time you sign in, you will be redirected to a page titled **Set up two-factor authentication**.

1. Open your authenticator app on your phone.
2. Tap the **plus** or **add account** button. Choose **Scan a QR code**.
3. Point your phone's camera at the QR code on the LS Nexus page. The app captures the code automatically.
4. Your authenticator app now shows a 6-digit code labelled **LS Nexus** (or **LS Nexus: your.email@lifescientific.com**) that changes every 30 seconds.
5. Type the current 6-digit code into the **Verification code** field on LS Nexus.
6. Click **Verify and finish**.
7. LS Nexus shows you **ten backup codes**. **Write them down** somewhere safe — see [Section 2.4](#24-backup-codes-save-these). You will not see them again.
8. Click **Done**. You will be redirected to the Dashboard.

If your authenticator app cannot scan the QR code (older app, no camera permission), every QR code page also displays the same secret as a long string of letters and numbers. Tap **Enter setup key manually** in your authenticator app and type the string in.

### 2.3 Daily sign-in with MFA

Every time you sign in:

1. Enter your email and password as usual.
2. Click **Sign In**.
3. LS Nexus shows the **Two-factor authentication** screen.
4. Open your authenticator app, find the **LS Nexus** entry, and type the current 6-digit code into the **Verification code** field.
5. Click **Verify**. You land on the Dashboard.

The 6-digit code changes every 30 seconds. If you start typing one and it expires, just type the new one — LS Nexus accepts the previous code, the current code, and the next code (a window of about ±60 seconds), so small clock differences don't lock you out.

### 2.4 Backup codes (save these!)

When you first enrolled, LS Nexus gave you ten **backup codes**. Each one is a single-use 10-character code. They look something like this:

```
A1B2-C3D4-E5
F6G7-H8I9-J0
... eight more ...
```

Use a backup code when you cannot use your authenticator app — most commonly because:

- Your phone is dead, lost, stolen, or being repaired.
- You're travelling and your phone has the wrong time zone or no signal.
- You replaced your phone and forgot to migrate your authenticator app.

To use one:

1. On the **Two-factor authentication** screen, click **Use a backup code instead**.
2. Type one of your unused backup codes (with or without dashes — LS Nexus accepts both) into the field.
3. Click **Verify**.
4. The code is **immediately consumed** and cannot be reused. Cross it off your list.

**Where to store backup codes:**

- A password manager (1Password, Bitwarden, LastPass) is ideal — encrypted, cloud-synced, searchable.
- A printed sheet kept in a locked drawer is acceptable.
- Do **not** store them in plain-text email, sticky notes on your monitor, or unencrypted files in cloud drives.

When you've used eight or nine of your ten codes, sign in normally and visit your account-settings page (top-right avatar → **Account settings**) to **regenerate** a fresh set. Regenerating invalidates the old set.

### 2.5 Lost or stolen phone

If you lose your phone or replace it without migrating your authenticator app, you have two options:

1. **Use a backup code** to sign in (see Section 2.4). Once signed in, go to **Account settings** and re-enroll: click **Reset MFA**, scan a fresh QR code on your new phone, and save a new set of backup codes.

2. **Contact your administrator** to reset your MFA. They will clear your enrollment in a few seconds. The next time you sign in, you'll be redirected to the QR-code enrollment page just like a first-time user.

If you have neither a backup code nor an administrator with access (e.g. you are the only administrator and you've lost both), see [Section 16.6](#166-im-the-only-admin-and-im-locked-out).

### 2.6 Account lockout

After **five failed sign-in attempts** (wrong password OR wrong MFA code), LS Nexus locks your account for **fifteen minutes**. You'll see the message:

> Your account is temporarily locked. Please try again later.

The lock clears automatically after fifteen minutes. If you remember your password during that time and want to sign in immediately, an administrator can unlock you faster.

## 3. Password and Account Management

### 3.1 Password requirements

LS Nexus passwords must be:

- At least **8 characters** long.
- Maximum **128 characters**.

We strongly recommend at least 12 characters with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols, ideally generated by a password manager. Reusing a password from another service is the single most common way accounts get compromised.

### 3.2 Forgot password flow

If you've forgotten your password:

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your email address. Click **Send reset link**.
3. Check your inbox for an email titled **Reset your LS Nexus password**. The link is valid for **one hour**.
4. Click the link. You'll land on a page asking for a new password.
5. Type your new password twice and click **Reset password**.
6. You'll be redirected to the sign-in page. Sign in with your new password.

The reset email comes from `noreply@ls-nexus.com`. If you don't see it within a few minutes:

- Check your spam / junk folder.
- Confirm with your administrator that your email address is spelled correctly in LS Nexus.
- Resend the reset link (it's safe to request multiple — only the most recent is valid).

The reset link is a one-time use. If you click it twice, the second click will fail. Just request a fresh link.

### 3.3 Changing your password while signed in

Click your avatar in the top-right corner → **Account settings** → **Change password**. Enter your current password once and your new password twice.

### 3.4 Session timeout

LS Nexus signs you out automatically after **8 hours** without activity. A warning appears 5 minutes before timeout — click **Stay signed in** to extend, or do nothing to be signed out.

If you close the browser tab, your session is still valid for the same window — opening a new tab to ls-nexus.com within 8 hours will not require you to sign in again.

### 3.5 Avatar and profile photo

To change the photo shown on your account:

1. Click your avatar in the top-right → **Account settings**.
2. Click your avatar again on the settings page → **Upload photo**.
3. Choose a square JPEG or PNG. The image is stored privately and only visible to other LS Nexus users.

## 4. Navigating LS Nexus

Every page in LS Nexus has the same layout:

- **Sidebar (left)** — navigation between top-level features.
- **Header (top)** — page title, search box (when applicable), and primary action buttons (e.g. "New Customer").
- **Main area (centre)** — the content of the page.
- **Avatar menu (top-right)** — account settings and sign-out.

### 4.1 Sidebar overview

The sidebar lists every top-level area of the application:

| Sidebar item | What it is | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Your home screen. KPIs, revenue trends, top customers, upcoming actions. | Everyone |
| **Customers** | List and details of every customer account. | Everyone (filtered by role) |
| **Pipeline** | Kanban board of in-flight sales deals. | Everyone (filtered by role) |
| **Item Master** | Product catalogue (SKU, name, category, price, UOM). | Everyone |
| **Forecasting** | Build and review revenue forecasts. | Everyone (filtered by role) |
| **Account Scoring** | Numerical scores ranking each customer by priority. | Everyone |
| **Action Items** | Kanban board of tasks linked to customers. | Everyone |
| **Analytics** | Trend charts and rollup statistics. | MANAGER, ADMIN |
| **Users** | User provisioning and MFA management. | ADMIN |

What you see is filtered by your role:

- **REP** — only customers, deals, forecasts, and action items where you are the assigned rep.
- **MANAGER** — everything in your division (LS_US or LS_CANADA).
- **ADMIN** — everything across all divisions.

### 4.2 Searching

Most list pages have a search box at the top. Search is **case-insensitive** and matches partial strings. The search box on each page shows a placeholder telling you which fields it covers (e.g. "Search by name, account number, or industry").

### 4.3 Sorting and filtering

Most tables let you:

- Click a column header to sort ascending; click again to sort descending.
- Use a **Status** dropdown above the table to filter (e.g. **Active**, **Inactive**, **Prospect**).

### 4.4 Detail pages and tabs

Many records (customers especially) have a **detail page** with several tabs across the top. Click a tab to switch between related views without losing your place. The URL updates so you can bookmark a specific tab.

## 5. Dashboard

The Dashboard is your home screen and the first page you see after signing in. It is a read-only summary of your work — every panel is a shortcut to a deeper section of the app.

### 5.1 KPI cards

The top row shows four cards summarising your key metrics for the current period:

- **Total Customers** — how many customer accounts you (or your division, depending on role) own.
- **Active Forecasts** — forecasts in flight for the current quarter.
- **Open Action Items** — tasks not yet marked Done.
- **Pipeline Value** — total dollar value of deals in non-terminal stages (everything except Won and Lost).

Click any card to jump to the corresponding list page filtered to the same scope.

### 5.2 Revenue Chart

A line chart of historical revenue over the trailing twelve months, broken out by month. The y-axis is dollars; the x-axis is calendar month. Hover any point to see the exact figure for that month.

The chart is scoped to your role: REPs see only their own assigned customers' revenue; MANAGERs see their division; ADMINs see everything.

### 5.3 Top Customers

A table of your highest-revenue customers, ranked by trailing-12-month total. Each row shows:

- Customer name (clickable; takes you to that customer's detail page).
- Total revenue.
- Star rating (a manual 0–5 score you maintain on the customer record).

### 5.4 Upcoming Action Items

The next five action items due, sorted by due date ascending. Click any item to jump to its full record.

### 5.5 Pipeline Summary

A small bar chart counting deals in each stage (Lead, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, Lost). Click a bar to filter the Pipeline page to that stage.

## 6. Customers

The Customers section is the heart of LS Nexus. Every other feature ultimately ties back to a customer record.

### 6.1 Customer list page

The Customers list shows every customer you have access to. Each row contains:

- Customer name.
- Account number (auto-assigned if you didn't supply one — see Section 6.4).
- Industry.
- Type (Distributor, Retailer, Direct, Other).
- Status (Active, Inactive, Prospect).
- Star rating (0–5).
- Trailing-12-month revenue.
- Assigned rep.

Search by name, account number, or industry using the search box at the top. Filter by status using the dropdown.

> **REP only** — you only see customers where you are the assigned rep. To get access to additional customers, ask your manager to reassign or copy you in.

### 6.2 Creating a customer

Click **New Customer** in the top-right of the Customers list page. Fill in the form:

- **Name** — required. The customer's legal or trading name.
- **Account number** — optional. Leave blank to have LS Nexus generate the next sequential number automatically. If you supply one, it must be unique.
- **Industry** — optional. Free text (e.g. "Agricultural distribution", "Commercial nursery").
- **Type** — Distributor (default), Retailer, Direct, or Other.
- **Wholesale %** and **Retail %** — split of the customer's business between wholesale and retail channels. Should sum to 100. Used in forecasting.
- **Rating** — your subjective 0–5 priority rating. Drives sorting on the Dashboard's Top Customers panel.
- **Status** — Active (default), Inactive, or Prospect. Inactive customers are hidden from forecasting and the dashboard but remain searchable.
- **Notes** — free-text scratchpad. Visible to anyone with access to the customer.
- **Website** — optional URL.
- **Assigned Rep** — the user this customer belongs to.
- **Division** (ADMIN only) — LS_US or LS_CANADA. Determines which managers and reps can see the customer.

Click **Save** to create. You'll be taken to the new customer's detail page.

### 6.3 Editing or deleting a customer

On the customer detail page, click **Edit** in the top-right to change any field. Click **Delete** to remove the customer permanently.

> **Caution** — deleting a customer also deletes their locations, contacts, action items, forecasts, revenue records, and attachments. This is irreversible. If you don't want the customer to appear in lists but want to keep their history, set status to **Inactive** instead.

### 6.4 Auto-assigned account numbers

If you leave the Account Number field blank when creating a customer, LS Nexus generates the next sequential number in the format `LS-NNNNNN` (e.g. `LS-001234`). The numbers are assigned at creation time and are sequential across all divisions.

If two people create customers at exactly the same instant, one of them may be retried automatically with the next number — this is invisible to you and you will always get a unique number.

### 6.5 Customer detail page tabs

Every customer record has a detail page with six tabs:

#### Locations

Physical addresses where the customer operates — warehouses, retail stores, growing operations. One customer can have many locations. Click **Add Location** to create one. Fields:

- Name (e.g. "Main warehouse", "Regina branch")
- Address line 1 / 2
- City, State/Province, Postal code, Country
- Latitude / longitude (optional, for mapping)
- Notes

Locations are not used for billing — they're for sales planning ("I'm visiting Regina next week, what locations are nearby?").

#### Contacts

People at the customer's organisation you do business with. Click **Add Contact**. Fields:

- Name (required)
- Title (e.g. "Purchasing Manager")
- Email
- Phone
- Is Primary — flag the main decision-maker. Each customer can have only one primary contact.
- Notes

The primary contact appears on the customer summary card across the app.

#### Revenue

Historical revenue records and a line chart. Each revenue record links to a date and a dollar amount, optionally broken out into individual line items by product. Used to populate the Top Customers panel and the Revenue chart on the Dashboard.

Revenue is normally imported in bulk from finance — most reps will only view this tab, not edit it.

#### Action Items

The Kanban board of tasks scoped to this customer. See [Section 11](#11-action-items) for full details on action items. From the customer's Action Items tab you can:

- See every task tied to this customer.
- Click **New Action Item** to add one (the customer is pre-filled).
- Drag a card between To Do, In Progress, and Done.

#### Forecasts

All forecasts associated with this customer. See [Section 9](#9-forecasting). From this tab you can create a new forecast for the customer with the customer pre-selected.

#### Quotes

Quote documents tied to the customer. Currently used to attach prepared quotes for review and tracking; the formal quote-generation flow is on the roadmap.

### 6.6 Star ratings

The 0-to-5-star rating on each customer is **subjective and rep-set**. There is no algorithm computing it. Use it however your team has agreed — common patterns:

- 5 stars: Strategic top-tier accounts. Visit monthly.
- 4 stars: Important accounts. Visit quarterly.
- 3 stars: Steady mid-tier. Visit semi-annually.
- 1–2 stars: Long-tail. Touch annually.
- 0 stars: Inactive, prospect, or unrated.

The Dashboard's **Top Customers** panel sorts by trailing-12-month revenue, not stars — but you can filter the Customers list by rating to find your high-priority accounts.

### 6.7 Wholesale / retail channel split

Each customer has a **Wholesale %** and **Retail %** field that should sum to 100. These percentages drive how forecasts split a single forecast amount between wholesale and retail revenue. A purely-wholesale distributor would be 100/0; a direct-to-end-user customer would be 0/100; a hybrid might be 70/30.

Set these correctly when you create the customer; you can update them later but new forecasts will use the current split.

## 7. Pipeline

The Pipeline section tracks deals — sales opportunities you are actively pursuing — through six stages. It is a Kanban board with one column per stage.

### 7.1 The six stages

| Stage | Default probability | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | 10% | Initial contact made. Customer has expressed interest but no specific opportunity is defined yet. |
| **Qualified** | 25% | We have confirmed the customer has budget, authority, need, and timeline. |
| **Proposal** | 50% | We have submitted a written proposal or quote. |
| **Negotiation** | 75% | Customer is actively negotiating terms. |
| **Won** | 100% | Deal closed. Money is committed. |
| **Lost** | 0% | Deal lost or abandoned. |

Stages have default probabilities used to weight pipeline value (a $100k deal at Proposal stage counts as $50k in weighted pipeline). You can override the probability on individual deals if a particular customer's situation warrants it.

### 7.2 Creating a deal

Click **New Deal** in the top-right of the Pipeline page. Fields:

- **Title** — short description (e.g. "Q3 Spring Distribution — Greenfield Co").
- **Customer** — must be an existing customer in LS Nexus. Type to search.
- **Stage** — defaults to Lead.
- **Value** — dollar value of the deal (your best estimate).
- **Probability** — defaults to the stage's default (see table above). Override if you have better information.
- **Expected close date** — optional but strongly recommended.
- **Owner** — defaults to you.
- **Notes** — free text.

Click **Save** to create. The deal appears in the Lead column of the Kanban board.

### 7.3 Moving deals through stages

Drag a deal card from one column to the next. Or open the deal and change its stage in the form. When a deal moves to **Won** or **Lost**, LS Nexus prompts you for a closure note ("Why did we win?" / "Why did we lose?"). These notes feed into the Analytics section.

### 7.4 Email notifications

When a deal is created, edited, or moved to Won/Lost, LS Nexus sends an email summary to:

- All ADMIN users.
- All MANAGERs in the same division as the deal's customer.

This keeps managers informed without them having to check the app every day.

> **REP only** — you don't get these emails for your own deals. They're for management oversight. You can subscribe yourself by asking an admin to add your email to the notification list.

### 7.5 Won deals and revenue

When you mark a deal **Won**, LS Nexus does **not** automatically create a revenue record on the customer. Revenue records are imported separately from finance to ensure the booked-revenue column matches the books.

If you want to track expected revenue from a won deal, leave the deal as Won and use Forecasting (Section 9) to project the timing.

## 8. Item Master

The Item Master is the product catalogue — every SKU LifeScientific sells. Most users only **view** this list; product creation and editing is restricted.

### 8.1 The list page

The Item Master page is a sortable, searchable table. Each row shows:

- **SKU** — the product's stock-keeping-unit code (unique).
- **Name** — the product's display name.
- **Category** — e.g. Herbicide, Fungicide, Seed treatment.
- **Unit Price** — list price per unit.
- **UOM** — unit of measure (e.g. L, kg, gal, lb).
- **Status** — Active or Discontinued.

Search by SKU, name, or category. Click a row to see the product's full details, including:

- A description.
- Linked attachments (label PDFs, MSDS sheets — see [Section 14](#14-file-attachments)).
- A list of forecasts and quotes that reference the product.

### 8.2 Adding or editing a product

> **ADMIN / MANAGER only** — only managers and administrators can create or edit products.

Click **New Product**. Fields are the columns above plus a description. The SKU must be unique.

Discontinued products are hidden from the forecasting product picker but remain visible on the Item Master page so historic forecasts and quotes still resolve.

### 8.3 File attachments on products

Each product can carry attachments — typically the printable label PDF, the SDS / MSDS sheet, and the product brochure. Users can download attachments without administrator privileges. See Section 14 for how attachment links work.

## 9. Forecasting

Forecasting is how reps and managers project future revenue per customer for a given period (typically a quarter or a year).

### 9.1 What a forecast is

A forecast is a record that says: "I expect Customer X to buy approximately Y dollars of these specific products during this period." It has these top-level fields:

- **Customer** — the customer being forecasted.
- **Period** — start and end date.
- **Status** — Draft, Submitted, Approved, or Closed.
- **Owner** — the rep building the forecast.
- **Notes** — free text.

Inside the forecast you add **forecast items**, each pinning a specific product to an expected quantity and price.

### 9.2 Building a forecast

1. Go to **Forecasting** in the sidebar (or the **Forecasts** tab on a customer's detail page).
2. Click **New Forecast**.
3. Pick the customer (or it's already filled if you started from a customer's detail page).
4. Set the period start and end date.
5. Add forecast items: pick a product from the catalogue, enter expected quantity and unit price. The line total computes automatically.
6. Add as many line items as you need.
7. Save as Draft.

### 9.3 Channel splits

Each forecast inherits its customer's wholesale/retail split (see Section 6.7). The forecast totals page shows the total broken out by channel — e.g. a $100k forecast on a 70/30 customer would show as $70k wholesale and $30k retail.

You cannot change the split per forecast — it's a property of the customer. If a single customer has different splits in different periods, create a separate customer record (rare).

### 9.4 Forecast statuses

- **Draft** — your working copy. Not visible in rollup reports.
- **Submitted** — you've finished and submitted to your manager for review.
- **Approved** — manager has signed off. Counted in division-level rollups.
- **Closed** — period has ended. The forecast is locked from further edits and used for historical comparison.

Move between statuses using the dropdown in the top-right of the forecast detail page.

### 9.5 Editing approved forecasts

Once approved, only the forecast's manager or admin can move it back to Draft or Submitted to make changes. Reps cannot edit approved forecasts. This is intentional — approved forecasts feed division-level commitments to LifeScientific corporate.

### 9.6 Comparing forecasts to actuals

The Analytics section ([Section 12](#12-analytics)) shows forecasts laid alongside actual revenue records once finance imports them. The variance column tells you whether you over- or under-forecasted.

## 10. Account Scoring

The Account Scoring page ranks every customer by a numerical priority score. The score is computed automatically by the platform — you cannot edit it directly.

### 10.1 What goes into the score

The score combines several signals (the exact weighting is tuned over time):

- **Trailing-12-month revenue** — how much they've actually bought lately.
- **Star rating** — your subjective priority.
- **Action item activity** — how much engagement is happening.
- **Pipeline value** — committed in-flight deals.
- **Days since last contact** — recency penalty.

Higher score = higher priority. The page sorts highest-first by default.

### 10.2 Why this matters

Use the score to plan your week. If a 5-star customer hasn't been contacted in 90 days, their score drops sharply — that's your prompt to pick up the phone. If a 2-star customer is suddenly buying a lot, their score rises — that's a sign to invest more time there.

The score is not a direct instruction — it's a reminder system that surfaces accounts you'd otherwise forget about. Use your judgement.

### 10.3 Filtering and sorting

The Account Scoring page lets you:

- Sort by score (default), name, or last-contact date.
- Filter by division (MANAGER and ADMIN only).
- Search by customer name.

### 10.4 Acting on a score

Click a row to jump to the customer's detail page. From there, log a new action item, schedule a visit, build a forecast — whatever the situation calls for. Logging activity bumps the score back up.

## 11. Action Items

Action Items are tasks. Each one is a thing you (or a teammate) need to do, optionally tied to a customer.

### 11.1 The Kanban board

The Action Items page is a three-column Kanban board:

- **To Do** — not started yet.
- **In Progress** — actively being worked.
- **Done** — completed.

Drag a card between columns to update its status. Or click the card and change the status field directly.

### 11.2 Creating an action item

Click **New Action Item**. Fields:

- **Title** — required. Short summary of the task.
- **Description** — optional. Longer context.
- **Customer** — optional. Linking to a customer makes the task appear on that customer's detail page Action Items tab.
- **Assignee** — defaults to you. Reassign to anyone else by typing their name.
- **Priority** — Low, Medium (default), or High.
- **Due date** — optional but recommended.
- **Status** — defaults to To Do.

Click **Save**. The card appears in the appropriate column.

### 11.3 Priority

Priority is purely informational — there is no automated escalation. The card colour and label change based on priority (High items have a red badge, Medium grey, Low outlined). The Dashboard's Upcoming Action Items panel sorts within a due-date by priority descending.

### 11.4 Email notifications

When an action item is **assigned to you** by someone else, you get an email. The notification CC's the LS Nexus operations team for visibility. Self-assignments do not generate emails.

### 11.5 Weekly digest

Every Monday at 8:00 AM UTC, all MANAGERs and ADMINs receive a digest email listing every action item still in **To Do** or **In Progress** status — sorted by due date, oldest first. This gives leadership a single weekly view of what's outstanding without anyone having to ask.

### 11.6 Bulk operations

Currently the board does not support multi-select. To process many items, use individual drag-and-drop. Bulk operations are on the roadmap.

## 12. Analytics

The Analytics section is a rollup of the data across the rest of the application, intended for sales managers and administrators rather than individual reps.

> **MANAGER and ADMIN only** — this section is hidden from REPs.

### 12.1 What's on the page

Analytics is a series of charts and tables showing:

- **Forecast vs. actuals** — per-customer or per-rep variance for the current and previous period.
- **Pipeline movement** — deals entering and leaving each stage week-over-week.
- **Win/loss ratio** — deals won vs. lost, with closure-note text-cloud.
- **Rep activity** — action items closed per rep per week, customer touches per rep per month.
- **Page-view tracking** — which areas of LS Nexus are being used and by whom (operational metric, not a sales metric).

### 12.2 Filtering

The page-level filters at the top let you scope every chart to:

- A division (LS_US, LS_CANADA, or all — ADMINs only).
- A specific rep (within your division for MANAGERs).
- A date range.

### 12.3 Exporting data

Charts can be saved as PNG images by hovering over a chart and clicking the camera icon. Tabular data can be exported to CSV with the **Export** button above each table.

### 12.4 Page-view tracking

LS Nexus records which page each user visited and when. This is used by the operations team to spot stuck workflows ("nobody is using the Forecasts tab — is something broken?"). It is **not** used for performance reviews.

You can see your own page-view history on the **Account settings** page. You cannot see anyone else's.

## 13. User Management (ADMIN)

> **ADMIN only** — this entire section applies only to administrators.

The Users page lets administrators provision new accounts, edit existing ones, deactivate users, and reset MFA.

### 13.1 The Users list

A table of every user in LS Nexus. Columns:

- Name
- Email
- Role (ADMIN, MANAGER, REP, CS_REP)
- Division (LS_US, LS_CANADA, or none)
- Status (Active, Inactive)
- MFA status (Enrolled, MFA pending)
- Last sign-in (TODO: not currently displayed)

### 13.2 Creating a user

Click **New User**. Fields:

- **Name** — required. Full name as it should appear in the UI.
- **Email** — required. Must be unique. The user's sign-in identifier and where reset emails go.
- **Password** — required. Set a random temporary password and share it with the user via a secure channel (Signal, password manager share, in-person). They can change it after first sign-in.
- **Role** — required.
  - **ADMIN** — full access. All customers, all forecasts, all users. Use sparingly.
  - **MANAGER** — full access within their division. Can read other reps' data.
  - **REP** — sees only their assigned customers.
  - **CS_REP** — customer-service rep; same as REP for now (reserved for future read-only/customer-service-specific view).
- **Division** — required for MANAGER and REP. ADMINs can be cross-division.
- **Region** — free text. Used for sales-team grouping and on emails.
- **Active** — defaults to checked. Uncheck to deactivate without deleting.

Click **Save**. The user can sign in immediately with the password you set; they will be forced through MFA enrollment on their first sign-in.

### 13.3 Editing a user

Click the pencil icon on any user row. You can update every field except email. To change a user's email, contact the development team — it requires a database update.

### 13.4 Deactivating vs. deleting

Use **Deactivate** (uncheck the Active checkbox) when someone leaves the company. Their account becomes unable to sign in but their data (assigned customers, forecasts, action items) remains intact and queryable.

There is no **Delete user** button. Deleting a user is a manual database operation handled by the development team. If you need a user permanently removed (e.g. for GDPR right-to-be-forgotten compliance), open a ticket with engineering.

### 13.5 Reassigning customers

When a rep leaves or changes territories, their customers need to be reassigned. From the user's edit page click **Reassign customers**, pick the new owner, and confirm. Every customer record updates atomically. Forecasts, action items, and revenue records still reference the original creator; only the **assignedRep** flips.

### 13.6 Resetting a user's MFA

If a user loses their phone, has used all their backup codes, or is otherwise locked out of MFA:

1. Find them on the Users page.
2. Click the pencil icon to open their edit form.
3. Click **Reset MFA**.
4. Confirm. The user's MFA secret and backup codes are deleted immediately.
5. The user signs in with their existing password and is taken straight to the QR-code enrollment flow.

You don't need to reset their password as part of this — only the MFA state.

### 13.7 Resetting a user's password

The Users page does not currently have a password-reset button. Use one of:

1. **Ask the user** to use the Forgot Password flow (Section 3.2). This is the preferred path.
2. If the user can't access their email (e.g. email is mis-spelled in LS Nexus), contact engineering to use the password-reset script.

### 13.8 Roles cheat-sheet

| Permission | REP | MANAGER | ADMIN |
|---|---|---|---|
| View own customers | yes | yes | yes |
| View other reps' customers in same division | no | yes | yes |
| View customers in other division | no | no | yes |
| Create customers | yes | yes | yes |
| Delete customers | own only | division | all |
| Create / edit products | no | yes | yes |

## 14. File Attachments

Many records in LS Nexus support file attachments — customers, contacts, products, forecasts, and quotes most commonly. Files are stored privately in cloud storage and only accessible to LS Nexus users who have access to the parent record.

### 14.1 Uploading a file

On any page that has an **Attachments** section:

1. Click **Upload file** or drag the file onto the page.
2. Wait for the upload progress bar to complete.
3. The file appears in the list with a download icon.

There is no enforced file-type restriction, but per LifeScientific data-handling policy, **do not upload**:

- Customer credit-card numbers, bank routing numbers, or any other PCI data.
- Personal identifiable information (SSNs, passport numbers, driver's licence images).
- Anything covered by HIPAA.

Documents OK to upload: signed contracts, quote PDFs, label PDFs, MSDS sheets, photographs of product damage, scans of purchase orders.

### 14.2 File size limit

The current per-file limit is **50 MB**. For larger files (high-resolution video, large CAD drawings), upload to your team's shared Drive and paste the link as a note instead.

### 14.3 Downloading a file

Click the download icon on the attachment. LS Nexus generates a private download link valid for one hour. The link cannot be shared with people outside LS Nexus — even if they have the URL, the link is signed against your session.

### 14.4 Deleting a file

Click the trash icon next to the file. A confirmation prompt appears. Once deleted, the file is removed from cloud storage immediately and cannot be recovered.

## 15. Tips and Best Practices

### 15.1 Daily routine

Most reps find this 15-minute morning routine works well:

1. Open LS Nexus → Dashboard.
2. Glance at **Upcoming Action Items**. Knock out anything overdue.
3. Click **Top Customers**. Pick one you haven't touched recently and call them.
4. Check **Account Scoring**. Anyone red? Action item them.
5. Open **Pipeline**. Move any deals that progressed yesterday.

That's it. Everything else is reactive.

### 15.2 Weekly routine

- **Monday morning** — manager-issued action items digest hits inboxes. Read it before standup.
- **Friday afternoon** — review your forecasts. Submit any drafts that are ready. Clean up the action item board so the digest next Monday isn't full of stale items.

### 15.3 Naming conventions

When you create things, future you and your teammates have to find them later. Be consistent:

- **Customers** — use the customer's actual legal name. Avoid abbreviations unless they're standard (e.g. "PepsiCo" not "Pepsi Co.").
- **Deals** — `<Period> <Product Family> — <Customer>` (e.g. "Q3 Spring Distribution — Greenfield Co").
- **Forecasts** — `<Period> <Customer>` (e.g. "Q3 2026 Greenfield Co"). LS Nexus does this automatically if you don't supply a name.
- **Action items** — write them as imperative sentences that name the verb. Good: "Send Q3 quote to Mary Chen at Greenfield". Bad: "Greenfield".

### 15.4 Keep the data clean

- A customer's status should reflect reality. Inactive ≠ not buying right now; Inactive = no longer a customer at all. Use Prospect for not-yet-buying, Active for currently-buying, Inactive for gone.
- A deal that's been sitting in Negotiation for 90 days probably isn't really there. Move it forward, lose it, or downgrade to Lead.
- An action item without a due date is an action item that won't get done. Always set one.

### 15.5 Notes are free; use them

Every record has a Notes field. Use it. The single most common complaint about CRMs is "I don't remember why I set this". A two-line note when you create or edit a record solves this forever.

## 16. Troubleshooting

### 16.1 I forgot my password

Use the Forgot Password flow (Section 3.2). The reset email comes from `noreply@ls-nexus.com`; check your spam folder if you don't see it within five minutes.

### 16.2 My MFA code is rejected even though I'm sure it's right

Most likely cause: your phone's clock is drifting. The 6-digit code is computed from the current time, so a phone that thinks it's 11:42 when the world thinks it's 11:43 will produce wrong codes.

Fix: on iPhone, **Settings → General → Date & Time → Set Automatically (on)**. On Android, **Settings → System → Date & time → Use network-provided time (on)**.

LS Nexus tolerates ±60 seconds of skew, so if you're within a minute of correct, the code will still be accepted.

Less common cause: you're entering the code from a different LS Nexus authenticator entry. If you re-enrolled MFA recently, your authenticator app may still show the **old** entry — the codes from it look identical (six digits) but won't validate. Delete the old entry and use only the most recent one.

### 16.3 I'm locked out

After five failed attempts you're locked for 15 minutes. Wait it out, or ask an administrator to unlock you immediately.

### 16.4 The page is loading forever

Hard-refresh the page: `Cmd+Shift+R` on macOS, `Ctrl+Shift+R` on Windows. If that doesn't work, sign out and back in. If still broken, it may be a system-wide outage — check `https://status.ls-nexus.com` (TODO: not yet live) or ask your administrator.

### 16.5 I can't see a customer I'm sure exists

Two possibilities:

1. You're a REP and the customer is assigned to a different rep. Ask your manager to reassign or add you.
2. The customer is in a different division. Only ADMINs see across divisions.

### 16.6 I'm the only admin and I'm locked out

This is the worst case. If you have **even one** unused backup code, sign in with that and re-enroll MFA immediately.

If you have no backup codes and no working authenticator, contact the development team. They can clear your MFA at the database level — see [docs/engineering-onboarding.md §9](./engineering-onboarding.md#9-operations-and-runbooks) for the procedure. This will require you to verify your identity through an out-of-band channel before they touch anything.

To prevent this from ever happening:

- Always have at least one other ADMIN, ideally two.
- Print your backup codes and put them in a safe.
- When you replace your phone, re-enroll MFA on the new phone **before** retiring the old one.

### 16.7 My emails (forecasts, digests, notifications) aren't arriving

Check your spam folder for messages from `noreply@ls-nexus.com` and `digest@ls-nexus.com`. If you find them there, mark them as Not Spam and add the addresses to your address book or safe-senders list.

If they're not in spam either, your email provider may have blocked them at the gateway. Ask your IT department to allowlist `*@ls-nexus.com`.

### 16.8 The data I see seems wrong

Most "wrong data" reports are actually filter/scope confusion:

- The Dashboard shows your scope, not the company-wide scope. As a REP, your "Total Customers" KPI is your customers, not LifeScientific's.
- A 0 on the Revenue chart means no revenue records exist, not that revenue is zero. Revenue records are imported from finance — if finance hasn't imported the latest month yet, it'll look like a drop.

If you've ruled out scope and you genuinely think a number is wrong, contact your administrator with: the page URL, the specific field, and what you expected to see vs. what you saw.

## 17. Glossary

- **Account number** — a unique identifier per customer, in the format `LS-NNNNNN`. Auto-assigned at customer creation if you don't supply one.
- **Action item** — a task. May be tied to a customer or standalone. Tracked on a Kanban board.
- **ADMIN** — administrator role. Full access across all divisions. Can provision users and reset MFA.
- **Authenticator app** — a smartphone app that generates 6-digit codes used as the second factor of MFA. Examples: Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password, Authy.
- **Backup code** — a single-use 10-character code that substitutes for an authenticator-app code when the app isn't available. Each user gets ten at MFA enrollment.
- **Customer** — a company that buys products from LifeScientific. The central entity in LS Nexus; everything else hangs off customers.
- **CS_REP** — customer-service rep role. Currently identical to REP; reserved for future read-only views.
- **Deal** — a sales opportunity tracked on the Pipeline board. One customer can have many deals.
- **Division** — LS_US or LS_CANADA. Determines which managers and reps can see a record.
- **Forecast** — a structured projection of expected revenue for a customer over a period. Has a status (Draft, Submitted, Approved, Closed) and contains forecast items.
- **Forecast item** — a single line within a forecast: one product, one quantity, one price.
- **Item Master** — the product catalogue.
- **MANAGER** — manager role. Sees everything in their division.
- **MFA** — multi-factor authentication. The 6-digit-code-on-phone process required at every sign-in.
- **Pipeline** — the Kanban board of in-flight deals.
- **REP** — sales representative role. Sees only their assigned customers.
- **Revenue record** — a real, booked dollar amount of business with a customer in a given period. Imported from finance, not usually edited in LS Nexus.
- **Star rating** — your subjective 0–5 priority score for a customer.
- **Stage** — one of the six pipeline columns (Lead, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won, Lost).
- **TOTP** — Time-based One-Time Password. The cryptographic algorithm that generates the 6-digit MFA codes. RFC 6238.
- **Wholesale / Retail split** — a customer-level percentage split (must sum to 100) that drives how forecasts attribute revenue to channel.

## 18. Getting Help

### 18.1 Self-service first

Most issues are answered somewhere in this manual. Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on macOS) is your friend.

### 18.2 Your administrator

For account-level issues — locked out, MFA won't work, can't find a customer, need a role changed — contact your administrator. They can:

- Unlock your account.
- Reset your MFA.
- Reassign customers.
- Change your role or division.
- Activate or deactivate accounts.

### 18.3 The development team

For platform-level issues — features not working, errors on the page, data that looks wrong, suggestions for new features — open a ticket with the development team. Include:

- The exact URL of the page where the issue occurred.
- The time it happened.
- A screenshot if possible.
- A short description of what you expected vs. what you saw.

### 18.4 Emergency contact

If LS Nexus is down completely or you suspect a security issue (someone using your account, a leaked password), contact the development team immediately by phone or whatever after-hours channel your organisation uses. Don't email a suspected account compromise — assume the email is being monitored.
